Order Pipeline

Team Roles
Built for every team member
👔
Team A — Admin
Full access. Receives orders, runs credit checks, assigns partners, and oversees the entire pipeline.
⚙️
Production Partner
Sees only their assigned orders. Marks production started and complete for efficient workflow management.
🔍
Quality Control
Reviews goods, passes or fails quality checks, and adds detailed QC notes for the production team.
💰
Billing & Dispatch
Creates invoices, marks orders dispatched, and automatically notifies clients on completion.
