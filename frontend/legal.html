1Introduction

Web Graphics Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the WGS Order Processing platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our service. By using WGS Order Processing, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and password (stored as a secure hash)
  • Company Information: Company name, workspace slug, and role within your organization
  • Order Data: Client names, contact details, order descriptions, amounts, and status history
  • Usage Data: Login timestamps, actions performed, and timeline entries
  • Google Account Data: Email address (only if you connect Google Drive for backups)
3How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to operate the WGS Order Processing platform:

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session securely
  • To manage and track orders through the workflow pipeline
  • To send transactional notifications (order status updates via email or WhatsApp)
  • To enable automated cloud backups to your Google Drive
  • To provide role-based access control across your team
🔒 We do not sell, rent, share, or use your data for advertising or marketing purposes.
4Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on MongoDB Atlas (a secure cloud database). We implement the following security measures:

  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt — never stored in plain text
  • Authentication via industry-standard JWT (JSON Web Tokens)
  • Role-based access control limiting what each user can see and do
  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Company-scoped data isolation — each company's data is completely separated
5Google Drive Integration

If you choose to connect Google Drive for backup functionality:

  • We request only the drive.file scope — access limited to files created by this app only
  • We do not access, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive
  • Your Google OAuth refresh token is stored securely in our database
  • You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from the Backup & Restore settings
6Notifications

By providing your email address or phone number, you consent to receiving transactional notifications related to your orders — such as order status updates, dispatch confirmations, and quality check results. These are operational notifications, not marketing communications. You may opt out by contacting your system administrator.

7Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your company workspace remains active. You may request deletion of your personal data or your entire company workspace by contacting your administrator or reaching us directly. Backup files stored in your Google Drive are under your own Google account's control and can be deleted by you at any time.

8Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data (use the Backup & Restore → Download feature)
  • Correct: Update your profile information via the Edit Profile option
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Export all your company data in JSON format at any time
